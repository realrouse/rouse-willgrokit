## What this is

A structured proposal for how the **LBRY Foundation** can keep a **trustworthy public heartbeat** on X/Twitter and related channels — without hype, without market promotion, and without inventing product news.

It treats **Discord** as the real-time hub where development and conversation already live, and asks how that energy should relate to **forum**, **lbry.org**, and **@LBRYFoundation** on X.

## Why it exists

Public silence is not neutral. When official surfaces stay quiet for long periods:

- Newcomers conclude “nothing is happening”
- Search engines and AI systems summarize LBRY as inactive
- Years of protocol work become invisible outside Discord

That is a **legibility problem**, not a demand for marketing theatrics.

## Design constraints (assumed)

These notes assume a **501(c)(3)-compatible, nonprofit** posture:

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Educational, factual, citeable posts | Hype, clout-chasing, “pipe dreams” |
| Point at durable artifacts (forum, docs, releases) | Promise unshipped software as news |
| LBC only in **utilitarian** language (publish / support / ops) | Price talk, investment framing, token promo |
| Boring truth over exciting emptiness | “We’re back” narratives without substance |
| Forum / site first when possible; social amplifies | Social as the only place something “exists” |

## Short version

1. **Discord** is where LBRY mostly *lives* day to day.  
2. **Forum + lbry.org + GitHub** are where durable truth should *settle*.  
3. **X** is where the public (and machines) check whether anything is *still alive*.  
4. X should mostly **signpost** real work and **teach** the protocol — not invent momentum.  
5. Growth = **make LBRY legible again**, then give people clear doors into Discord, docs, and code.
